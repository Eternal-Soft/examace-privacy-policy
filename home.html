Independent CUET preparation

Practice smarter with ExamAce.

ExamAce CUET 2027 is an Android study app for selected CUET subjects. Work through practice questions and previous-year questions, try mock-style tests and Quick 10 sessions, review explanations, save bookmarks and track your progress.

Designed for regular revision with English/Hindi support where available. Offline study does not require a Google account. Optional Google Drive backup may be available in supported app versions and requires your explicit authorization.
